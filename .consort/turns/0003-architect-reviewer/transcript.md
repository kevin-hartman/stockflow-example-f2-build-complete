## Final reasoning

I annotated all three S1-file-stock acceptance criteria and wrote `architecture.json` and `architecture.md`, and the self-check now passes. The first self-check failed on the NFR field names; I fixed them and re-ran it.

- **Layers per AC:** AC1-file-stock-record and AC2-retrieve-stock-record are `E2E`, each with its seams from `ia.md`. AC3-collision-resolved-at-write is `API`, because the "re-file updates in place" rule needs its own backend test.
- **Project layout:** `service_backed` is true, so this layout becomes the project-wide convention:
  - routes in `app/routes/` (React SPA, JSON only)
  - business logic in `app/services/`
  - database access in `app/repositories/`
  - models as a package in `app/models/`
  - engine and connection in `app/database.py`

  The scaffold's flat `app/models.py` has to become that package during the build.
- **Database guarantees** (7, on `stock_records`): (sku, location) is unique, sku/location/quantity are required, quantity can't be negative, a re-file is one atomic upsert, and the create-table migration can be rolled back and re-applied. `tracking_code` can be empty, meaning "not tracked".
- **Endpoints:** file stock (`POST /api/stock`), look up one record (`GET /api/stock/record`) and list locations (`GET /api/locations`). Listing distinct locations is declared as a new query shape, so the DBA can decide on an index.
- **NFRs:** every Required item R1–R5 is covered. Two parts of the brief can't apply to this feature, which only records and reads stock. One is R1's "unmodifiable timestamp and actor on every adjustment". The other is R2's "no overcommit". Both are marked deferred to the adjustment and pick features.
- **Decisions for you to confirm at the design gate:**
  - **Locations:** there's no separate locations table. The list is every distinct location in `stock_records`. An "empty location" is one whose records all have quantity 0.
  - **SKUs:** there's no SKU catalog. Filing a SKU for the first time creates it.
  - **Re-filing:** filing an existing (sku, location) replaces the quantity rather than adding to it. Later receive and adjust features will need their own operations that add or subtract.

Every NFR is marked `accepted`, as headless mode requires.
